# Attack record: dispersed translations and their Boolean cost

- **Adversarial roles.** The lead derived physical incidence. A separate skeptic and compiler analyst checked its canonical-owner cases and final row split. The dense-mask author attacked the packed sampler; its author independently checked the dense-mask interpolation and quantifiers. The lead read the proofs and code and integrated scope repairs. These are separate model work streams, not outside semantic review.
- **Fresh bits versus root bits.** Only original tokens enlarge a seed reader coset. Fresh padding is enumerated inside a call. A deeper column stops at its own sibling and cannot secretly return to an earlier owner. Root cells, nonrectangle row cells and rectangle cells have different one/two-coset cases. Full reader sets were compared at k7..10; the k12 sharp-cell control is explicitly sampled outside its predicted reader set.
- **Radius correction.** An earlier safe weight4a bound can be sharpened to3a because the last row split retains at most a original tokens. The packed checker now prints the actual weight8 at k160. A reported possible contradiction with12 was a confusion between a safe bound and a measured maximum.
- **Boundary root overwrite.** B must have full row rank. Its odd majority groups give exactly D sites per region. Interpolate on the entire region, not just interior centers. If an interior view reads root(y), it intersects y's own view, proving y remains in the same region. This is the reason arbitrary boundary symbols can be restored.
- **Dense is not unbiased.** Full rank, density and pairwise distance do not bound every character. Triple parities see just one flip. Arbitrary weights still have a character of bias at least1−2/m. Conversely this criterion is only necessary for a useful family; it is not a full characterization of soundness.
- **Packed parameters.** Coefficient packing remains injective with an incomplete final block. The least-m rule must be used outside the inverse-polynomial regime. The Fourier argument uses multiset weights even when the AND deduplicates masks. Two consecutive complete blocks determine both nonzero parameters. At large k the resulting physical views are disjoint; small instances really can pool.
- **Compiler overreach repaired.** Replication can amortize large shared overhead, so universal worsening is false. Independently scored copies with freely falsifiable shared seed pins need a separate lower argument. The exact t+1 sensitivity belongs to the stated decision-table toy; extra base gates can only justify a lower bound without further work. The toy does not independently force a NO decision table and is not a full recursive PCP.
- **General statements versus checks.** Large binomial counts do not instantiate a 2^k-sized proof. The k160 sampler audit constructs masks and displacement sets only. All four scripts use checks active under -O. Generic written arguments, exact finite replays, source attribution and any remote runtime evidence are reported separately.

The failed density and free gate-sharing routes are preserved in graveyard. No new circuit lower bound, barrier escape, novelty priority or Lean result is asserted.
