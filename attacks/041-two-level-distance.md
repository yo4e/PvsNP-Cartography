# Adversarial review of two-level distance and accepted-site agreement

- **Zero-distance loophole.** Use a separate polynomial root count on the actual external word. The three equations for nonconstant candidates have at most 31, 31 and 32 roots. Constants are handled separately. The single-bad-coset instance also gives a positive-distance equality witness for the repaired verifier. [Distance certificate](../expeditions/041-two-level-distance/counterexample.md)
- **Detached storage or trivial tests.** Trace physical aliases through both layers, including the second redirect to an external cell. The degree-31 positive control makes all 832 leaves nonconstant while passing all of them. Interpolate at the actual leaf abscissae. [Physical geometry](../expeditions/041-two-level-distance/oracle-geometry.md)
- **Sparse and odd branches.** Zero or one recovered row does not justify two-row interpolation. With a cubic outer column the threshold is four recovered rows. Below the corresponding threshold, or with at most two successful low groups, interpolate the accepted sites directly. Above it, the XOR involution guarantees at least four groups. [Both branches](../expeditions/041-two-level-distance/family.md)
- **Incorrectly paired constraints.** Only the RS part is paired. The outer residual's accepting set need not be invariant under the translation. Restrict the argument to the RS successes, then intersect with the original residual. A one-bit change in an already excluded coset can preserve the sharp NO minimum. [Outer application](../expeditions/041-two-level-distance/outer-application.md)
- **Changing the cost model.** Two calls preserve public randomness and individual address-map fibers. Counting field seeds gives no bit-level clause denominator. The growing-rate two-level family already incurs exponentially many inner-row symbols in the root dimension. [Cost boundary](../expeditions/041-two-level-distance/family.md)

These are author/team derivations and executable controls. No external semantic review or proof-kernel verification of this work was performed.
