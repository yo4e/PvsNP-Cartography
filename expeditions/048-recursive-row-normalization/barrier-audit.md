# Barrier audit — normalized recursive rows

## Exact scope

The positive construction is a public reindexing of a specified recursive PCP
address geometry. Its written cost analysis and row-polynomial collapse use
the source hypotheses of Expedition 038 and the hierarchy/counting ledger of
Expedition 039. The finite field application replays an already defined,
residual-constrained two-level example. It does not compile an arbitrary
machine into Boolean clauses or prove new circuit lower bounds.

## Relativization

The coordinate bijection, row interpolation and degree comparisons are
combinatorial/algebraic statements about explicit finite functions. They do
not themselves separate complexity classes. The conditional hierarchy
consequence invokes the unrelativized projection PCP and exact counting
theorems; we have not established their needed forms for arbitrary common
oracles. No first nonrelativizing step sufficient for P versus NP is claimed.
The Baker--Gill--Solovay barrier is neither contradicted nor escaped by
renaming a proof address.

## Natural Proofs

The local property under examination is approximability of every fixed row
by seed polynomials under an explicit weighted physical error budget. Its
application is to a narrowly defined proof/certificate class for a unary
exponential-time hierarchy language. No constructive, large truth-table
property useful against general polynomial-size circuits has been supplied.
Exact polynomial degree can be computed from a truth table, but that fact
alone supplies neither largeness nor usefulness. The Razborov--Rudich
cryptographic assumption and the three separate tests remain necessary for
any later general-circuit argument.

## Algebrization

The recursion, field checks and polynomial counting use algebra. That is not
evidence of a failure to algebrize. We have not proved an implication under
the asymmetric Boolean/algebraic oracle access of Aaronson--Wigderson, nor a
step that fails there and yields a P-versus-NP separation. Disposition for a
broad separation route remains **unknown**.

## Other limits that change the research decision

- The rate exponent `a` must stay fixed. The polynomial row-count bound has
  constants depending on `a`; it does not repair Expedition 046's growing
  one-level parameter regime.
- A cheap table on normalized transverse rows is feasible, while a separate
  guessed label for every physical tree node can still be exponentially long.
- A row-wise degree-`d` certificate subsumes every degree-`d` global polynomial
  after a tag-only relabeling. The direct row tuple is already short enough
  for the same counting clock. Layout search therefore adds no accepting
  power to that algorithm.
- A small general address circuit can raise composed degree to `k`. Its short
  description is not the missing counting theorem.
- The actual two-level YES field witness has affine seed rows. This does not
  establish the corresponding statement for Boolean gate rows or for
  arbitrary accepting witnesses of the source language.
- The finite degree certificates concern an artificial physical assignment;
  their free-padding and used-error conventions are explicit.
