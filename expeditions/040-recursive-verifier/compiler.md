# From shared field cells to fixed signed clauses

The YES instance uses the three shifted A addresses and signs of Expedition 039, and likewise for NO. Only the field and the B cutoff have changed. With H={0,1,2,3}, write Z_S for the monic vanisher of S. The two singleton selectors are Z_{1,2,3} and Z_{0,2,3}. Their cubic factors are (A(x)+1)²(A(x+j)+1) and A(x+i)³, where (j,i) is (1,0) on YES and (0,1) on NO. Add the range term Z_{0,1}(x) A(x+2)(A(x+2)+1) and quotient term Z_H(x)B(x). The public predicate requires the residual to vanish, degree <2 for the entire A root, and the selected recursive leaves to pass their checks. In both cases A, B are external GF(128) words. Each recursive extension cell holds a full GF(16384) symbol. The actual YES witness is A(X)=1+X and B(X)=X, including its honest bivariate extension.

## Fixed addresses and row-specific arithmetic

The seven-bit public seed is z=u+8v in binary coordinates. A proof input in the circuit has a fixed physical tag for (block, page, bit) and coordinate z XOR delta. Each such read has rank seven; upper extension coordinates of a B root read are constant zero. A slot with another role can map to the same tag and coordinate. Gate outputs, constants and seed copies have distinct tags read at z. A physical variable in the DIMACS export is one plus the binary integer obtained by concatenating the tag and coordinate. All fourteen clamped public-bit unit templates have rank six. Bits beyond the used tags on the enclosing power-of-two cube can be filled arbitrarily.

The base A test orders the field by XOR offsets from z and uses the values at offsets 0 and 1 for its candidate affine polynomial. For the B row, offsets 0..7 are precisely the physical parent cells. In the coordinate X'=X+(β_v+u) these points are the field elements 0..7, independent of z. Compute the degree-<8 polynomial S in X' interpolating the eight answers. At row offset w=u'+8e₁+16e₂+32m, the test point in these relative coordinates is

    u' + e₁c₁ + e₂c₂ + m(c₀ + θ + 8v).

Here 8v denotes the high four base-field coordinates. For m=0, the comparison uses S directly. For m=1 it translates S by c₀+θ and then by each high-coordinate basis element whose public bit is set. The circuit implements coefficient translation by the binomial identity in characteristic two: the coefficient of X^j after translation receives a monomial coefficient of degree i whenever j is a binary submask of i, multiplied by the corresponding power of the shift. The three binary coefficient-update stages implement this expansion for i<8. The fixed row weights used for the initial parent offsets are valid only together with these shifts. Example: the identity root polynomial gives 384 at row 1, slot 32; blindly reusing row 0's full-row weights gives 392.

For each of the four checked column groups, the physical address has page 4+fresh, low coordinate u and high coordinate v XOR offset. The leaf ordinate is q(β_{v XOR offset})=q(β_offset)+q(8v), so its affine interpolation weights relative to offsets 0 and 1 are independent of v. The data can thus remain in offset order. Each row fresh tape permutes the same 64-point domain and has the same full-domain membership decision. Checking one normalized row once is sufficient. The four column tests use distinct abscissae and all four remain in the circuit.

## Clausification and verification

The generator reuses the AND/XOR equivalence clauses of Expedition 039, including when inputs are signed wires. It folds exact Boolean constants, reuses identical gates, pins its shared constant and requires the final output wire to be true. Public bit i is represented by a fresh proof cell at each z. The two unit templates for i read at the clamped seeds and require the clamped value, so each incorrect public bit incurs exactly two failures across the template–seed multiset. These equivalences permit arbitrary global assignments to the physical cells. The lower-bound argument and attainment are in [soundness.md](soundness.md).

The scalar reference checks absolute leaf points with a second finite-field implementation and Lagrange interpolation. The Boolean verifier evaluates its translated coefficient circuit, reading physical address blocks. The two compared all 128 seed verdicts for 142 global field-table pairs on each of YES and NO. Selected witnesses were also evaluated on every packed signed clause and every public pin. The separately compiled C++ reader takes only a DIMACS stream and a byte-per-variable assignment; it has no access to the field or wire layout. It counts satisfied clauses independently of the packed evaluator. Both exports are reproducible from source.
