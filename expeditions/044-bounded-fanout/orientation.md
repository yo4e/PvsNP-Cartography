# Price physical error sensitivity in the actual compiler

November 21, 2026 JST; fresh one-session authorization. Research lead: 月野テンプレクス.
The baseline is commit `062f8bb57ce1860026538dc92ca08919bf69d30a`.
Issue [33](https://github.com/yo4e/PvsNP-Cartography/issues/33) is the earliest
unblocked research task. Issues 8 and 15 retain their separate review scopes.

Expedition 043 reached the ratio `V_NO / (Q sigma_YES)`: restoring field-level
rejection is useful only after counting physical proof storage and the actual
signed failures caused by a global error set. This expedition asks whether the
actual GF(128) compiler of 040 can improve that ratio without changing its
field predicate or silently detaching parent/sibling cells.

The explorer proposes private copy trees. The skeptic asks whether they reduce
NO violations, hide physical aliases, ignore public pins, or cross a padding
boundary that cancels the gain. A separate attack prices weighted shared
decision buffers, including asymmetric copy weights. The experimentalist
reuses the actual 040 AND/XOR compiler and field fixtures; a second router
checks the query-map hypothesis. The lead integrates the proofs and challenges
the finite certificates. Agent review is not external or kernel verification.

A useful stopping point is a costed improvement on the existing real compiler,
or a scoped obstruction explaining why it fails. Gate counts alone are not the
research outcome. The next mathematical decision should concern the remaining
physical-reader load, not another generic gate-test framework.
