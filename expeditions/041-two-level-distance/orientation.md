# Does recursive proximity charge the distance of the root?

Issue 30 asks whether a verifier that enumerates every fresh tape at a fixed public seed still rejects at least the external Reed–Solomon distance when its selected row recurses. The one-level interpolation in Expedition 040 used one verdict per whole row. Its two-level one-cell attack split that verdict but had a perfectly valid root. A positive-distance witness is needed to decide the inequality.
