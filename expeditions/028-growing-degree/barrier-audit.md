# Barrier and scientific-scope audit

Author: 月野テンプレクス,2026-10-29. Same-author audit, not external review.

This expedition reconstructs the known finite-field approximation method's
square-root-scale probabilistic-degree obstruction. It is not a P-versus-NP
proof attempt promoted from finite tests. MOD3 and a bound on representing it
by low-degree randomized polynomials are not a lower bound for arbitrary
Boolean circuits or a running-time lower bound.

Relativization, natural proofs and algebrization: no escape from any of these
barriers is claimed. This work neither supplies a general-circuit usefulness
proof nor a largeness/constructivity analysis for a candidate natural property.
Using finite-field algebra alone is not evidence of bypassing algebrization.
The next restricted circuit bridge, if completed, would reconstruct a known
restricted-model consequence, not solve an unrestricted circuit problem.

Primary comparison: Goldreich, On teaching the approximation method for circuit
lower bounds, ECCC TR23-034 revision1 (March25,2023), Sections2.2.1,3.1-3.2 and
Footnote9. Read parsed primary PDF text; screenshot requests failed with cache
miss. No visual page inspection or local PDF archive is claimed. The text's
fixed polynomial averaged over inputs and its Boolean/residue conventions must
not be silently substituted for this repository's fixed distribution with a
pointwise input guarantee. Our explicit adapters discharge those differences.
No targeted priority search establishes novelty; the constant here is a loose
author reconstruction, not a literal attribution to the paper.

The research decision is to move from single-degree thresholds to a reusable
all-degree obstruction, then test the missing gate/circuit approximation
connection. Do not resume automatic n13 enumeration or unbounded audit tooling.
