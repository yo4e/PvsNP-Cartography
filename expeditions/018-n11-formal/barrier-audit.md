# n11 barrier and prior-art scope

This expedition proves or checks only a finite GF(2) MOD3 probabilistic-degree
statement on exactly11 variables. It supplies no asymptotic circuit lower bound,
no complexity-class separation and no proof-barrier escape.

Relativization: no arbitrary oracle argument or BGS escape is proposed.
Natural Proofs: no large constructive property useful against unrestricted
circuits is furnished; the cryptographic condition must not be dropped from
any future use of that barrier. Algebrization: using finite-field polynomials
is not an identified non-algebrizing step.

Primary context re-opened2026-10-20: Srinivasan, Tripathi, Venkitesh,
On the Probabilistic Degrees of Symmetric Boolean functions,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02465 . Its abstract defines pointwise random-polynomial
agreement and reports classification up to polylogarithmic factors. This is
context, not a novelty certificate or an independent verification of our n11 data.
Lean tactic documentation was also checked at
https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Tactic-Proofs/Tactic-Reference/ .
Actual project execution remains pinned to Lean4.35.0-rc4 and existing Mathlib,
not silently upgraded to the latest documentation's version.

Novelty stays unknown. No exact optimum, support minimality or all-n extension
is inferred from the finite result or the preceding n5..10 sequence.
