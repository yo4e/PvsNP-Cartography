# End-to-end base case for the projection verifier

**Scope.** Two explicitly generated instances start at the signed, translated-clause interface of BV14 Theorem 2.2. They apply the actual selector and quotient equations (2)–(5), both full root RS tests, and the physical Boolean clause construction. There is no Turing-machine-to-CSP reduction or recursive RS child in these instances. The field and cutoffs are chosen to exercise the base case and need no complexity-theoretic size premise. This is author-written finite mathematics with a reproducible checker, novelty unknown.

## Source instance

Use F=GF(32) in the binary basis modulo t⁵+t²+1. Integers denote coefficient bit strings. Set H₁={0}, H₂=H₀={0,1}, H={0,1,2,3}. The satisfying values of the three literals for β=0 are all one, and for β=1 they are all zero. The three XOR shifts for β=0 are (0,0,1) in YES and (0,0,0) in NO. For β=1 they are (0,0,0) in YES and (1,1,1) in NO. Thus the NO constraints force opposite values on the same variable. Range testing reads A(x+2). The quotient read B(x) uses a distinct block. All three read types use the same seed x.

Write Z_S(X)=∏_{s∈S}(X+s). The singleton selectors are Z_{1,2,3} and Z_{0,2,3}. The range selector is Z_{0,1}, and the quotient multiplier is Z_H. In each instance the residual is the sum of these four multipliers times, respectively, the three-factor literal product for β=0, the one for β=1, A(x+2)(A(x+2)+1), and B(x). This is characteristic two. Use strict degree cutoffs 2 and 4 for A and B, so the root code rates are 1/16 and 1/8. Dimension 5 is below both recursion stopping cutoffs 2a, a=4 and a=3. Each seed reads all root symbols using x XOR each offset. Membership is invariant under this translation, even on a noncodeword, since P(X+x) preserves the degree and translating back is inverse.

Source: Ben-Sasson and Viola, *Short PCPs with projection queries*, 9 February 2014, Theorem 2.2, Definition 5.1, §5.2 eqs. (2)–(5) and Claims 5.4–5.5; §6 base-case stopping rule. [Primary paper](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/017/download/)

## A sharp soundness calculation for the actual binary clauses

YES has the global witness A(X)=1+X, B(X)=X. Substitution cancels the residual as a polynomial: the singleton terms sum to (X+1)Z_H(X) and the range term is Z_H(X). The checker also reconstructs B by interpolation and evaluates all 32 seeds.

For NO, violation of either root degree test rejects at every seed. Otherwise the residual is a polynomial of degree at most seven: each singleton selector has degree 3 with three factors of degree at most 1, the range term has degree at most 4, and the quotient term has degree at most 4+3. Vanishing at both 0 and 1 would force A(0)=1 and A(0)=0. The nonzero residual consequently rejects at least 25 of 32 seeds for all possible A,B. The bound is attained. Set A=0 and let B have degree at most three interpolating 1/x at x=4,5,6,7. The residual is Z_{1,2,3} + B Z_H. Its seven roots include 1,2,3 and the four interpolation points; those are all its roots.

The generator bit-blasts the field arithmetic and two interpolation tests into XOR and AND gates, constrains both Boolean constants separately, and requires one acceptance-output bit. Physical root inputs have addresses (block, bit, x XOR offset); gate and public-bit cells have addresses (tag,x). All aliases resolve to the same physical variable. For bit i, the two additional signed unit-clause templates query the actual global cell at (public-i, clamp_i(x,e)) with required value e. Ordinary reads have rank five and pins rank four. Physical addresses use 3,858 tags of five coordinate bits, hence a 17-bit padded cube. The 123,456 physically used Boolean variables are shared across the 32 compiled seeds.

For every seed rejected on the true public bits, either an ordinary clause fails or at least one public input cell for that seed is wrong: otherwise gate induction forces the rejecting output. Across all seed-pin pairs each incorrect public cell fails exactly two pins, counting multiplicity. Thus the number of rejected seeds is at most ordinary failed pairs plus half the failed pin pairs. The NO witness with the sharp cubic B and honestly evaluated circuit wires fails exactly one output unit clause at each of 25 seeds. Consequently the minimum number of failed physical clause–seed pairs, over **all** global assignments to 123,456 bits, is **25 out of 469,056**. YES has minimum zero. Uniform selection from the 14,658 templates and 32 seeds gives these same fractions; clause occurrences are counted with multiplicity. The universal minimum follows from the algebra and gate induction, rather than enumeration of all proof tables.

## Computation and attacks

The generator emits the full DIMACS with `python verify_base_acsp.py --dimacs no` or `--dimacs yes`. With no options it checks the exact witnesses on the physical CNF and compares compiled verdicts with a separate scalar equation/moment-based RS predicate. This independent evaluation uses Σ_x x^j f(x)=0 for j=0,…,q−d−1: evaluating the unique representative of degree <q and extracting its highest q−d coefficients proves equivalence to degree <d. The two evaluators share the field representation and arithmetic. Both normal and optimized Python produced the saved JSON. Each instance checked 338 global A,B pairs at all 32 seeds, including every single root-bit flip of the YES witness, dense word pairs and additional codeword pairs. All truth-table entries of the individual gate templates were checked. These experiments do not independently verify a proof kernel.

**Failed shortcut.** The sentence about a selector evaluating to 1 in the proof of BV14 Claim 5.5 must be read using the nonzero condition of Definition 5.1. In this field Z_{1,2,3}(0)=6. The on-H vanishing argument needs only nonzero. Replacing selectors on the whole field with Boolean set indicators while keeping the honest source B invalidates the bit-verifier: at x=4 every such indicator is zero, while Z_H(4)B(4)=10·4=13 in GF(32), so the honest proof now fails. This is an implementation counterexample, not a refutation of the source theorem. The subsequent step for #28 is a generated instance with at least one recursive child, including field-valued overlaps and seed-dependent proof addresses through the redirects.

A closed form for the sharp witness uses U(X)=∏_{v=4}^7(X+v). The cubic B=(U(X)+U(0))/(X U(0)) has B(v)=1/v at each chosen v. With A=0 the residual is Z_{1,2,3}(X)U(X)/U(0). This factors the seven accepted field points without searching codeword pairs. The remaining 25 give the exact minimum above. Small-field direct root checks here do not exercise the auxiliary field dimension required for the recursive part of BV14 §6.
