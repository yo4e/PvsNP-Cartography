# Barrier audit and scope

The immediate objects are the verdict of one finite signed verifier and the uniform approximation of a particular encoded proof-bit family in E^NP by GF(2) polynomials. The hierarchy contradiction imports a projection PCP and exact counting. It yields a candidate implication for this restricted model at weak error. The checked finite examples diagnose encoding failures. They have no asymptotic extrapolation step.

- Relativization. The use of algebraic PCPs and nondeterministic simulation is specific to the cited machine models. There is no argument for a separation of P and NP in the presence of arbitrary oracles.
- Natural Proofs. The simulator guesses candidate polynomials to decide a fixed unary language. No constructive large truth-table property useful against unrestricted polynomial-size circuits has been established.
- Algebrization. Interpolation over finite fields and GF(2) root counting establish particular algebraic identities. Neither analyzes inclusions or separations against algebraic extension oracles.
- Complexity classes and external dependencies. The canonical bit function uses a deterministic exponential computation with an NP oracle. Input-length padding, physical addresses, occurrence tags and field-line encodings are explicitly charged. No transfer to NP, constant uniform error at the same degree, or independent proof-kernel verification is recorded. Model collaborators share research context and do not fulfill the external reviews in Issues 8 and 15.
