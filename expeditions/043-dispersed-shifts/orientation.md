# Which translated checks buy useful agreement?

November 18 JST, fresh approval. Starting main: `7348d52447f5007e20a94534734047ee140d9b38`. [Issue 32](https://github.com/yo4e/PvsNP-Cartography/issues/32) asks for a costed improvement or obstruction for approximate accepted-site agreement on the actual recursive query maps. The old low-rank and unit-vector attacks show that mask cardinality and rank alone are insufficient. The old constructive upper bound uses a scalar powering sampler. Its field gain has not been priced through the final Boolean verifier.

Keep the shared offset-address RS verifier, constant rate exponent a≥3, degree cutoff D=2^(k−a), and one physical proof for all public seeds and masks. Write L for raw symbol occurrences, u for distinct physical symbols in a base call, H for fixed XOR masks containing zero, and K for the exact base overlap displacements. Conditional accepted-site defect and additive excess for the once-only outer residual retain the separate definitions in Expedition 042.

Three bounded targets can decide the next research direction: test whether dense, mutually separated, full-rank masks repair the old attack; reconstruct the packed form of the known sampler and count its actual shared reads; determine whether private or shared Boolean decision gates preserve the numerator of the field improvement. A useful outcome is a cheaper explicit schedule or a failed design family with its cost and failure mechanism exposed. Optimizing an unrelated finite table is secondary.

The scientific continuation is to improve the uniform proof-bit approximation requirement in the existing one-global-polynomial reduction. A field-level improvement is an intermediate result. The final target must also account for the proof address width, literal signs, total shared-cell load and public seed pins.
