# Barrier and prior-art audit

Target: exact affine correctness under a fixed input distribution; convert
that bound to degree-one exclusion with the repository's original predicate.
This is not an unrestricted circuit lower bound or a P-vs-NP proof route.

Relativization: no oracle-machine separation is supplied. Natural Proofs:
no large constructive truth-table property useful against unrestricted
circuits is supplied. Algebrization: finite-field polynomial terminology does
not certify a non-algebrizing argument. No barrier escape is asserted.

Primary context checked2026-10-21: Srinivasan, Tripathi, Venkitesh,
On the Probabilistic Degrees of Symmetric Boolean functions,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02465 and https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1294162 .
The retrieved abstract uses pointwise high-probability agreement and reports
classification up to polylogarithmic factors. It does not establish priority
for the explicit dual here. Novelty remains unknown; no publication claim.

The generic proof must be structural. Bounded exact tests, self-review and
another CI runner are distinct from formal acceptance and human review (#8).
