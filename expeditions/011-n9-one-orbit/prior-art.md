# Prior-art positioning of the n=9 certificate

Targeted check on 2026-10-16 JST. Primary literature:
Srikanth Srinivasan, Utkarsh Tripathi and S. Venkitesh,
"On the Probabilistic Degrees of Symmetric Boolean Functions",
SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 35(3), 2070–2092 (2021).
https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1294162
Preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02465

The published abstract characterizes probabilistic degree for symmetric Boolean
functions up to polylogarithmic factors over fields of fixed characteristic.
It supports the general relevance and terminology, not the exact n=9 layer
table, the 4/3/2 seed, or minimality. A targeted public search did not
identify this exact small-n construction; that is NOT a comprehensive
priority/novelty audit or evidence of originality.

Our result is a precisely checked finite reproduction/construction in one field
and one arity. The arbitrary-arity statements used are generic elementary
permutation counting and duality bridges, not an all-n MOD3 degree theorem.
Novelty: unknown; independent human review: pending.
