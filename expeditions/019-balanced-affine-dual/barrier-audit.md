# Scope and barrier audit

Target: an explicit affine-score formula and a real algebra inequality, not a
superpolynomial lower bound. No arbitrary oracle separation is claimed;
there is no extracted large constructive truth-table property useful against
unrestricted circuits; no step is identified as non-algebrizing. Thus no
relativization, Natural Proofs or algebrization escape follows.

Literature context remains the primary symmetric probabilistic-degree paper
https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02465 , re-opened2026-10-20. It does not establish
novelty of this elementary counting/algebra manipulation. A restriction from
an already excluded small arity would also supply a degree-one obstruction
for larger arities once formally transported. The present contribution is
verification structure, not a claimed new asymptotic lower bound.
