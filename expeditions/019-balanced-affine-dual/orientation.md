Approved2026-10-20 session, begun while the n11 formal run executes. This is
an adjacent structural task, not a replacement for completing Issue #11.

Repeated finite lower certificates use equal total probability on Hamming
layers1 and3. Seek an explicit common score formula and a checkable algebraic
bound, rather than blindly extending finite tables to arbitrary arity.

Separate evidence classes: (a) informal combinatorial derivation; (b) exact
bounded validation; (c) a Lean theorem about the algebraic expression for all
real n>=7,0<=k<=n, with no counting identity assumed proved. The generic
Boolean-cube counting bridge is a future target, not a hidden premise.

The basic exclusion of degree1 at larger arities can also be inherited by
zero-coordinate restriction from an existing small-arity lower bound. Thus
this work is not claimed a new general complexity result; its value is an
explicit dual formula/gap and reusable audited algebra. No support optimality,
novelty, all-n degree2 upper bound, circuit separation or P-vs-NP claim.
