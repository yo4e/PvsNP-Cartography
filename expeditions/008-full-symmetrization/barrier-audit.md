# Barrier audit: generic full symmetrization

Approval night: 2026-10-13 JST. Role: skeptic/cartographer, same author.

## Exact scope

The result transports an existing finite-support probabilistic-polynomial
certificate, or a certificate with a bound on every full input orbit, into
one input-independent family with permutation-invariant success. It is a
symbolic theorem for arbitrary finite arity, not an extrapolation from tested
arities. It proves no new numerical MOD3 degree value or circuit lower bound.

## Relativization

No oracle-machine class relation is asserted. A finite group reindexing identity
does not provide a nonrelativizing complexity argument or contradict either
Baker-Gill-Solovay oracle world. There is no claimed barrier escape step.

## Natural Proofs

No truth-table property with asymptotic usefulness and quantitative largeness
against a circuit class is constructed. Enumerating finite examples in the
semantic audit supplies neither of these conditions. The generic averaging
identity cannot be substituted for a circuit-size lower bound.

## Algebrization

Renaming GF(2) polynomial variables is not evidence of nonalgebrization.
The proof contains no separation in the asymmetric algebraic-oracle framework.
A future P-vs-NP route must supply its own barrier audit at its decisive step.

The precise primary-source barrier statements and assumptions remain in
../../map/BARRIERS.md and ../../docs/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md. This expedition neither
weakens nor claims to evade them.

## Other boundaries attacked

The target must be symmetric; all permutations are not a cyclic subgroup;
every-input-orbit bounds are not a whole-cube average; probability division is
in R, not GF(2); totalDegree is the degree of the actual polynomial object.
Seven explicit invalid variants are preserved in ../../graveyard/007-symmetrization-hypotheses.md.

## Remaining mathematical interface

The general Hamming-layer counting identity has not been formalized here.
Permutation invariance alone is not a completed formal proof that the orbit
average equals the uniform mean on a Hamming layer. Equal fiber multiplicities
and layer coverage remain the next acceptance conditions before the compressed
n=8 certificate is integrated. Finite n=7 replication checks that formula only
for the specific data used; it is not the missing arbitrary-arity Lean proof.
