# Scope and proof-barrier audit

Target: a known-method agreement bound for actual bounded-total-degree
polynomials on a nonzero two-point grid, with a finite-real-mixture consequence.
This is not a complexity-class separation or a new circuit lower-bound method.

Relativization: no oracle-machine class relation is proved. The finite algebraic
lemma does not identify a non-relativizing step sufficient for P vs NP.
Natural Proofs: a low-degree agreement obstruction for this restricted model is
not a demonstrated constructive, large, useful property against unrestricted
P/poly. No cryptographic assumption is discarded and no barrier escape asserted.
Algebrization: using field extensions and polynomials is not evidence of escaping
algebraic oracle access. No non-algebrizing class-separation step is established.

Named use: complete the missing agreement-dimension ingredient of Expedition024's
MOD3 chain. Generic real amplification and GF4 restriction/encoding still need
their own faithful proofs before n299 may be called an end-to-end formal result.
No n12 degree determination, optimal threshold, novelty or P-vs-NP result follows.

Primary author source: Goldreich, ECCC TR23-034 revision1, Section3.1 proof of
Lemma4 and Section3.2. Parsed text read; screenshot requests failed. The finite
binomial formulation is the repository's explicit reconstruction of that known
method, not a quote of an uninspected original1987 theorem. Prior barrier sources
and exact limitations are retained in map/BARRIERS.md.
