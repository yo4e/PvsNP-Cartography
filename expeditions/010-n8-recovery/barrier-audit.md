# Barrier audit: n=8 recovery and compression

Date: 2026-10-16 JST. Role: skeptic/cartographer, same author.
Reference map: ../../map/BARRIERS.md and ../../docs/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md.

This session repairs a finite MOD3 theorem and a general combinatorial averaging
interface, then constructs another finite witness. It is not a complexity-class
separation argument. The following limits apply to both evidence tracks.

Relativization: no oracle-machine separation or identified nonrelativizing step
is supplied. A finite truth-table theorem cannot be promoted to a uniform
polynomial-time/non-polynomial-time classification.

Natural Proofs: no large constructive property useful against general circuit
families is established. Actual polynomial degree here is not a circuit-size
lower bound. No cryptographic hypothesis in the barrier map is removed.

Algebrization: working over GF(2) and formally checking degree does not identify
a nonalgebrizing step. No algebraic-oracle argument or barrier escape is claimed.

The all-arity result is only a permutation/layer averaging identity. It does
not give all-arity MOD3 degree2. The numerical theorem is explicitly n=8;
combined historical numerical Lean coverage is n=5,6,7,8. The159-row certificate
is an exact finite computational witness, not an asymptotic construction.

Scaling requires a fresh certificate or a genuinely universal obstruction at
the next arity. A failed candidate, sampled dual or optimizer timeout does not
provide that obstruction. Independent semantic review and novelty assessment
remain separate from kernel acceptance and exact finite arithmetic.
