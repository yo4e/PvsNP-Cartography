# Price transcript planting by the actual query-view overlap

Fix the shared coordinate and offset convention of Expedition 038. For one original call, let W_z be the set of all canonical physical *symbol* addresses read at z, pooling duplicates. The call reads the external root at z: follow row edges, take zero on every fresh pad and use leaf offset zero. Each return then redirects to its parent, restoring the saved high part.

## A translation action on shared addresses

Write an additive seed shift as g=(g_L,g_H) at an internal node. On a row edge send the high label v to v XOR g_H, and recurse in its child with the shift (g_L,0^a). On a column edge, XOR g_L into the low part of its label, keep its fresh bits, and recurse in the child with shift g_H. On a point of a terminal node take its XOR with the local shift. These rules also act on the named point of a nonterminal node. They compose by addition; each is an involution.

The rules descend to canonical physical addresses. A row-parent redirect tests only fresh suffix bits, unchanged by the shift. Returning restores the label with shift g_H and the low part with shift g_L, as in the parent. A column-to-row redirect changes its labels to the row whose high label is the child point and the within-row coordinate (low, fresh, 1). Acting first on the column shifts the former by g_H and the latter by (g_L,0^a), exactly as when acting on the resulting row. The same argument works at every ancestor. All fresh choices are still enumerated and an XOR leaf offset commutes with the local shift. Consequently the resulting permutation of the physical-address space sends W_z to W_(z XOR g). This concerns address incidences. The field interpolation predicates and values need not be invariant under the permutation.

Put K = {x : W_0 and W_x have a common cell}. Then the one-call overlap neighborhood of z is z+K, with binary addition throughout. For a fixed mask set H containing zero, its augmented view is the union of W_(z+h) for h in H, since its own external symbol is already read. Its overlap neighborhood is **exactly z+K+H+H**: the two unions meet precisely when one pair of constituent views meets. Every center has the same neighborhood size n_H = |K+H+H|. Without zero in H, adjoining zero gives a valid upper bound for the own-center augmented model.

In both [planting arguments](planting.md) replace the q² bound on the number of affected seeds by n_H, including [the outer residual](outer-planting.md). In particular some proof forces every single root polynomial to miss at least floor((N−D+1)/(n_H+1)) accepted sites. The estimate n_H ≤ min(N, |K|·|H+H|) depends on the mask set's sumset. For a subgroup of t masks this costs a factor t on the actual one-call overlap size, compared to the t² estimate from arbitrary slot pairs. Raw slots and predicate cost still have to be paid separately. Counting pooled cells does not implement a cheaper signed-projection circuit by itself.

## What finite overlaps decide

At k=8, a=3 the physical checker reads 604 distinct cells per base seed. Exactly 136 seed views meet any given base view. Comparing all 256 choices of second mask with all seed addresses shows that schedules with **1,144** distinct reads can have overlap neighborhoods of **144** or **256** seeds. Thus even at the same distinct-read count their capacity to shelter a planted transcript changes. For neighborhood 144 a one-center construction leaves at least 112 untouched accepting zeros, which exceeds D=32. A full neighborhood makes this disjoint-view lower bound vacuous; it cannot certify soundness. Three- and four-mask controls check the sumset law at every center.
