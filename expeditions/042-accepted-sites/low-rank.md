# Low-rank translations admit almost every seed on an arbitrary root

Fix the rate exponent a≥3 and a k-bit field seed. Let N=2^k, D=N/2^a, and let r be the binary rank of the span of the chosen fixed XOR masks. The following construction works for **every prescribed external word** f. If r=o(k), one shared proof for f passes the bundled RS test with probability 1−o(1). For polynomially many translated calls the verifier still has polynomial query and predicate cost. In particular every subgroup with polynomially many masks has r=O(log k); a general polynomial-sized list can have rank k.

Include zero in the mask list. If it was absent, use this stronger test for the construction: a pass still implies all originally specified calls pass. Let V_z be the resulting physical view. If V_z meets V_y, the [base displacement formula](exact-overlap.md) implies z+y is a sum of a mask-span vector and a vector supported on at most 4a original seed coordinates. This is only a necessary overlap condition; that direction suffices below.

## Equal-size interpolation regions

Divide k seed positions into m=floor(k/(r+1)) disjoint blocks of size r+1, discarding the remainder. In each block the restrictions of a basis of the mask span form a binary matrix with r rows and r+1 columns. Choose a nonzero vector in its kernel, extended by zero on all other blocks. The resulting m parities are independent unbiased bits at a uniform seed because their nonempty supports are disjoint. Every mask fixes all these parities. Flipping at most 4a original coordinates changes at most 4a of the parity bits in total.

Use a disjoint groups of n such parities each, where n is the largest odd integer at most floor(m/a). We consider k large enough that n≥1. The majority in each group is fair with no tie and the a majorities are independent. Their vector labels exactly 2^a equal regions of the external field, **each with D distinct sites**. Call a seed interior if every group weight differs from n/2 by more than w=4a. Any y with V_y intersecting its view lies in its own majority region: translations change no group bit and at most w other flips cannot cross any majority threshold.

For each region R interpolate f on all its D sites by a single polynomial of degree <D over the external field. Use its honest shared recursive proof on every cell read by any interior center in R. Interior views from different regions cannot overlap, so this instruction is consistent. Assign f on every external root site, including the boundary, and assign remaining auxiliary cells arbitrarily. To check this last step, if an interior center z reads root(y), then V_z meets V_y since the original call at y reads root(y). Hence y lies in z's region and its honest polynomial there already takes the prescribed value f(y). Every interior center sees the complete honest view for its region. Perfect completeness gives acceptance.

## Rate and two adversarial targets

Let p_n=2^(−n) choose(n,floor(n/2)). At most 2w binomial weights in one odd-sized group are within w of n/2. Therefore the boundary fraction is at most 2aw p_n; the exact interior fraction is the a-th power of the one-group interior probability. For n=2j the central mass equals the product of (1−1/(2i)) for i=1,...,j, bounded by exp(−Σ 1/(2i)) ≤ (j+1)^(−1/2). The maximum mass for n=2j+1 is at most the one at 2j. At fixed a, the constructed proof thus has rejection O_a(sqrt((r+1)/k)) whenever r=o(k).

- **A far root.** Prescribe f(x)=x^D. Every polynomial of degree <D can agree in at most D places by the nonzero difference polynomial. Interpolation on any D sites attains this. The exact relative distance is 1−2^(−a), at any k in scope. As r=o(k), almost every seed can nevertheless pass. In particular uniform distance domination by any positive constant cannot hold at arbitrary depth for the unmodified schedule or a polynomial-size subgroup. At two levels the separate 2/3 bound applies.
- **A designed NO instance.** Fix A=0 and prescribe B(x)=1/x for x≠0 and B(0)=0. Its physical residual is T₀(X)(1+XB(X)); it vanishes at every point except zero. Joint rejection for this single proof is at most 2aw p_n+1/N and tends to zero. For a genuine degree-<D B and any legal affine A, joint acceptances have the earlier D+3 cap. This construction preserves all shared proof cells and the original once-evaluated residual. It neither contradicts the inverse-polynomial fallback nor applies to the full-rank small-bias repair.

This is an existence construction; interpolating a proof table with exponentially many cells is external prover work. [Binomial cost/count controls](majority-checks.json) · [Small-bias alternative](outer-and-sampler.md). For k=65,536, a=3 and rank 2 the exact interior-count lower bound exceeds 47.18% of all field seeds. This number counts interior sites by integer arithmetic, without materializing that field. For fixed a, a guarantee of constant rejection on *every* far root at all sufficiently large dimensions requires mask rank Ω(k): any subsequence with r/k→0 gives the counterexample.

**Full rank alone also fails.** Take the k+1 fixed masks consisting of zero and every individual coordinate flip. Their span is the whole seed space. Any two masks differ in at most two positions, so every view-neighbor displacement has weight at most 4a+2 without quotienting at all. Use the original independent seed bits as checks and repeat the equal-region proof with w=4a+2 and the largest odd n≤floor(k/a). The same arbitrary prescribed root is accepted at 1−O_a(1/sqrt(k)) of seeds. This example isolates the need for dispersion of the actual mask schedule beyond the dimension of its linear span.
