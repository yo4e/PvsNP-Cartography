# Probabilistic-degree context recheck

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: definition and novelty boundary for finite n=8.

Primary sources read:

1. Srinivasan, Tripathi, Venkitesh, On the Probabilistic Degrees of Symmetric
Boolean Functions, FSTTCS 2019, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2019.28.
Definition 1 on page 28:2 specifies finite-support random polynomials,
pointwise error, and a support-wise degree cap. This is exactly the
quantity used in Expedition 004, not real uniform approximate degree.
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2019.28

2. Srinivasan, A Robust Version of Hegedus's Lemma, with Applications,
arXiv:2202.04982 (version accessed 2026-10-09).
Definition 2.3 on page 8 again fixes the pointwise convention. The paper's
introduction reports tight bounds up to constants for symmetric functions
in fixed positive characteristic, improving the earlier polylogarithmic gap.
Thus citing only the older characterization is incomplete asymptotic context.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.04982

Both cited definition pages were also inspected as rendered pages.
Their asymptotic bounds do not by themselves supply the exact small-n
certificate used here. A targeted search for probabilistic degree, MOD,
quadratic and 8 did not locate an equivalent exact table. This limited
negative search does NOT establish novelty. The witnesses may be known,
and external mathematical/prior-art review has not been obtained.

No claim of escaping a proof barrier or resolving P vs NP is supported
by the finite certificate. No publication/submission was performed.
